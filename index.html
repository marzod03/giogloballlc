About Us

Gio Zamora
Founder and Lead Journalist

Gio Global LLC is an independent, digital-first news organization founded and led by journalist Gio Zamora, based in Los Angeles, California. We deliver relevant, timely, and rigorously verified information tailored for Mexican and Latino communities across the United States and Mexico.

Operating without a traditional newsroom, we embrace a decentralized model that allows us to reach our audiences directly through powerful digital platforms. Our reporting combines deep investigative work with clear, direct, and courageous storytelling grounded in real-world experience.

Our mission is to empower the Mexican diaspora with the accurate information they need to stay informed, connected, and represented in the sociopolitical landscape of both countries. At Gio Global LLC, human journalistic judgment and integrity guide every story we tell.
